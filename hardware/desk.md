# Aesthedes 2 desk

The desk is connected to the central unit via three flat cables and some power wires.

See the [parallel port pinout](cards/aes_io/README.md)

The cables don't seem to have any labeling, but they each transport different kinds of data:

* Bitpad (digitizer / mouse) movements, from desk to central unit;
* Keyboard presses, from desk to central unit;
* Keyboard LEDs, from central unit to desk.

## Bitpad

The bitpad sends sequences of six bytes over port A, strobed on CA1. Either all six bytes are zero (meaning unclear), or they represent a movement event:

|     | 7   | 6   | 5   | 4   | 3   | 2   | 1   | 0   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0   | NC  | C4  | C3  | C2  | C1  | C0  | X14 | NC  |
| 1   | X13 | X12 | X11 | X10 | X9  | X8  | X7  | NC  |
| 2   | X6  | X5  | X4  | X3  | X2  | X1  | X0  | NC  |
| 3   | NC  | NC  | NC  | NC  | NC  | NC  | Y14 | NC  |
| 4   | Y13 | Y12 | Y11 | Y10 | Y9  | Y8  | Y7  | NC  |
| 5   | Y6  | Y5  | Y4  | Y3  | Y2  | Y1  | Y0  | NC  |

* NC: do not care
* Cn: buttons state
* Xn: X coordinate
* Yn: Y coordinate

## Keyboard

The keyboard sends 12-bit keycodes: higher 4 bit in lower nibble on port A, the remaining on port B. They are strobed on CA1.

The mapping of keycodes and command is listed in [keycodes.md](keycodes.md).
